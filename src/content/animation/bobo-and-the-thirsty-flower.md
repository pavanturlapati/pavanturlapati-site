A flower needs help, and Bobo and Little Star are there to lend a hand. They water the dry soil, wait patiently, and watch as the flower slowly lifts its head. A butterfly arrives to share the happy ending.

This is my first complete one-minute animated story, following the 15-second experiment that introduced these characters. I wanted to tell a gentle story about kindness, patience, and noticing the small things around us.

The voices of Bobo and Little Star are AI-generated using OpenAI.
