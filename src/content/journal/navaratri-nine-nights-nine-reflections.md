Navaratri, "nine nights", is a festival devoted to the Divine Mother in her nine forms. It is a time of worship, discipline and inner reflection, and this year I am reading it alongside Vedanta.

Each day I am writing one reflection on my Vedanta site, and I will keep adding to the series through the nine days. The articles are AI-assisted personal study notes, and the site says so on each page.

## Read the series

- [**Navaratri series hub**](https://vedanta.pavanturlapati.com/reflections/navaratri/) has every article in one place and is updated as new days are published.
- [All reflections in one place](https://vedanta.pavanturlapati.com/reflections/navaratri-all-reflections/) gathers every day's questions and answer boxes on a single page.
- [Navaratri: Nine Nights, Two Ways of Understanding](https://vedanta.pavanturlapati.com/reflections/navaratri-nine-nights-vedanta-and-tradition/) is the introduction.
- [Day 1: Shailaputri and the Foundation](https://vedanta.pavanturlapati.com/reflections/navaratri-day-1-shailaputri-foundation/) asks what it means to begin on firm ground.
- [Day 2: Brahmacharini and Steady Effort](https://vedanta.pavanturlapati.com/reflections/navaratri-day-2-brahmacharini-steady-effort/) looks at the story of Parvati's penance and *tapas* as the steady training of the mind.
- [Day 3: Chandraghanta and Calm Courage](https://vedanta.pavanturlapati.com/reflections/navaratri-day-3-chandraghanta-calm-courage/) looks at two traditional stories and at fearlessness as a calm, steady mind.
- [Day 4: Kushmanda and the Source of Light](https://vedanta.pavanturlapati.com/reflections/navaratri-day-4-kushmanda-source-of-light/) looks at the cosmic egg created by a smile and at the idea that our light is borrowed.
- [Day 5: Skandamata and Nurturing Care](https://vedanta.pavanturlapati.com/reflections/navaratri-day-5-skandamata-nurturing-care/) looks at the story of Skanda's birth and at care held without possession.
- [Day 6: Katyayani and Righteous Resolve](https://vedanta.pavanturlapati.com/reflections/navaratri-day-6-katyayani-righteous-resolve/) looks at the warrior form, the sword and the lotus, and desire and anger as the inner opponent.
- [Day 7: Kalaratri and Facing the Dark](https://vedanta.pavanturlapati.com/reflections/navaratri-day-7-kalaratri-facing-the-dark/) looks at the fierce form with the hand that says "do not be afraid", and at the move from darkness to light.
- [Day 8: Mahagauri and Letting Go](https://vedanta.pavanturlapati.com/reflections/navaratri-day-8-mahagauri-letting-go/) looks at the radiance uncovered after penance and at letting go as a way of receiving.
- [Day 9: Siddhidatri and Fulfilment](https://vedanta.pavanturlapati.com/reflections/navaratri-day-9-siddhidatri-fulfilment/) looks at the giver of *siddhis* and at fulfilment as knowing what is already whole.
- [Day 10: Vijayadashami and the Victory Within](https://vedanta.pavanturlapati.com/reflections/navaratri-day-10-vijayadashami-victory/) is a closing reflection on the tenth day, its stories and customs, and victory as mastery of the inner opponent.
